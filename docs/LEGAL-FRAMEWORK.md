# Sentire Framework: what Sentire may and may not call it

Not legal advice; the owner decides. The full note lives in the framework repo
(docs-sentire/LEGAL.md); this is the Ledger summary.

- Ledger runs on **Sentire Framework**, Sentire's own copy of Frappe Framework (MIT).
  Frappe's MIT licence and copyright line stay in the framework; Sentire's line is added.
- Users see **Sentire Ledger** everywhere (B38). The framework is named only in About
  (licences section) and in the BIR registration pack, as
  "Sentire Framework v16.60.2-sentire.1 (based on Frappe Framework 16.60.2)".
- Allowed: "Sentire Framework"; "based on Frappe Framework"; "compatible with ERPNext".
- Not allowed without Frappe's written permission: "Frappe" in a product, company or domain
  name; Frappe's logos in Sentire branding; anything implying Frappe endorses Sentire.
- ERPNext (GPLv3) stays upstream and unmodified. Its TRADEMARK_POLICY.md also forbids
  using "ERPNext" in advertising, including search ads: keep it out of Ledger's marketing.
