# Sentire Ledger user guide (short)

- **Invoices.** Make a Sales Invoice; the VAT class comes from the item and can be changed per line; VAT and the customer's withholding are computed. Submit to issue: the BIR serial is taken at submit and the posting date is today. Print gives ORIGINAL and DUPLICATE; the second print says REPRINT. Corrections: a Credit Note (decrease) or a new invoice (increase); an issued invoice is never edited or cancelled.
- **Collections.** A Payment Entry of type Receive is the Collection Receipt. If the customer paid net of withholding, enter the amount received; the withheld part is booked automatically. Record the 2307 the customer sends as a PH 2307 Received.
- **Bills.** A Purchase Invoice computes input VAT and the withholding from the supplier's category. If you must not withhold, tick the flag and give the reason. Advances to suppliers withhold at payment.
- **Period-end.** PH Compliance Calendar shows what is due. Compute each return's figures (PH Tax Return Figures) and generate the alphalist files (PH Alphalist File); run each file through the BIR Validation module on a Windows PC and record the result; copy the figures into eBIRForms; tick "filed" with the confirmation reference. Issue the 2307s to suppliers from the quarter's bills.
- **Books.** Print or export any book from Reports (PH General Journal and the others); every print carries the BIR header. At year-end, download the e-books pack from the Company form.
- **Closed periods.** Ask the Accounting Manager to create an Accounting Period after the quarter is filed; nothing can then be posted into it.

## Getting started: the Sentire Ledger workspace (B34)

The **Sentire Ledger** workspace is the first page after sign-in. Its card "Get to know Sentire Ledger" walks through the seven steps a Philippine business takes before issuing its first invoice: set up the Philippine company, choose the modules it uses, confirm the tax rules, print the first BIR invoice, compute the first BIR return, export the books, build the registration pack. The registration pack includes the notice of third-party custody the client files with its RDO, because the provider keeps its records (RR 9-2009 Sec. 6.3.1); fill in "Where the records and backups are kept" in PH CAS Settings first. Below the card are shortcuts to the daily documents and the BIR forms, and the books, tax and registration links.

After sign-in you land on Home: a greeting, the six module tiles, the create actions, the "Get to know Sentire Ledger" checklist (until done or dismissed), and the "Business at a glance" cards for the period chosen at the top right (this month, last month, this quarter, this year, all time). Every card opens the list or report behind it. The column of icons at the far left is the module switcher: hover it and the names appear beside the icons. The menu next to it collapses to an icon rail with the arrow at its lower edge or with Cmd+/ (Ctrl+/ on Windows), and opens again while you hover it.

The sign-in page, the icon in the left rail, the browser tab, the help menu and every email carry the Sentire Ledger mark; the help menu's last item is a contact link to the provider. The About entry under the help menu shows the product version and the open-source licences the system is built on (B38).

Tutorial videos (B51): the 38 Sentire Ledger tutorials play inside the system, from our own video server; nothing loads from YouTube. A step of the "Get to know" guides that has a tutorial shows its thumbnail beside the text, and plays it in place (with captions; the full-screen button makes it bigger). On Home, a step with a tutorial has a Watch button. Forms with tutorials (BIR Returns, the Sales Invoice, the Company, and so on) list them in the form's menu as "Watch: <title>", which opens the player in a window. The list Tutorial Videos (PH Tutorial Video) holds the 38 videos: which onboarding steps and forms show each one, and whether its files are on the server. A System Manager uploads them there: Upload videos, then drop the files (each video's .mp4, its .srt captions and its .png or .jpg thumbnail; the V code in the file name says which video a file belongs to). The files go straight to the private video server; one upload serves every client site. Check the server refreshes the list after another site uploaded. The video links are signed for a few hours at a time, so a copied link stops working.

## Modules: switch off what you do not use (B52)

Settings > Modules has one switch per optional module: Selling (quotations and sales orders), Buying (purchase orders and supplier quotations), Stock (receipts, deliveries, stock entries and counts), Manufacturing, Projects, Assets, Quality, CRM and Support. Accounts, BIR and the Home page are always on, and so are the masters every business needs: customers, suppliers, items, price lists and warehouses. A module that is off disappears from the module switcher, the sidebars, the search bar and the "Get to know" guides; a bookmark or link to one of its pages shows "This module is switched off" and goes Home. Nothing is deleted: switching it back on brings back every record. Pick an Industry on the same page to switch on the modules that industry usually needs (for example Stock for a shop, Projects for a contractor); you can change any switch afterwards. The "Choose what you use" step of the Sentire Ledger guide opens this page. Only a System Manager changes the switches.

## Loose-leaf books (B50)

For a client that keeps loose-leaf books instead of registering the CAS. On the Company, fill in "Loose-leaf books permit to use no." and its date. Then Company > Sentire Ledger > Loose-leaf books (Excel / PDF): choose the period (usually the calendar or fiscal year) and the format. The download holds the six books (Sales, Purchase, Cash Receipt, Disbursement, General Journal, General Ledger) and PAGES.txt with each book's page count. Print every page as it is (legal size, landscape), bind each book, and use the page counts in the sworn statement. The Excel and PDF files have the same pages; the Excel file keeps real numbers so it can be checked with formulas.

## Approval chains (B45)

Settings > Approval Chains. Nothing is on by default: switch the module on in Settings (Modules > Approval chains) and enable a chain per document type (Sales Invoice, Purchase Order, Journal Entry, any posted document), for one company or all. A chain is any number of steps; each step names its recipients (a role, a named user, a group, or a field of the document such as the cost center's owner, looked up in Approval Lookup), its receiving order (sequential, any one, or everyone), whether it approves or is only notified, whether the requestor may decide it, an optional condition that skips the step (two steps with opposite conditions branch by amount), and a private note the recipients see. Templates ("Manager approves above an amount", "Supervisor, then manager by amount band", "Everyone in a group") give a disabled chain to finish.

- On a document a chain applies to, Submit is replaced by **Request approval**. The recipients of the first step get an e-mail and the request on their home page under Awaiting your approval; they open the document and Approve or Reject (a rejection needs a reason and returns the document to the maker, who edits and requests again). The last approval posts the document: the posting event, the BIR series number, the event log with every approver.
- A change to the document after the request voids the request. The maker may withdraw a pending request. A site with one user, or a form with no chain, posts as before.
- A filed BIR return changes only through the chain on PH Tax Return (one step, Accounts Manager, by default): **Request change** with a reason; the approval opens the amendment. Without a chain, an accounts manager opens it directly.

## BIR Returns (B40)

Tax returns > BIR Returns is where a return is made, previewed, exported, filed and kept. One record per company, form and period (period keys: `2026-03` for a month, `2026-Q1` for a quarter, `2026` for a year; a fiscal-year company's quarters count from its fiscal year start on the Company record).

- **What the return still needs** lists, as questions, what is missing on the Company record (TIN, RDO, registered address, contact number and so on) or on the return itself. Open the record it names, fill it in, and the question disappears on the next save. Nothing the books already give is asked for.
- **Figures** shows every item of the form in the paper's order with its kind: books (summed from posted records; Refresh from books brings them in again), typed (yours), derived (computed, never stored). A books figure you change by hand is kept and flagged.
- **Preview (DRAFT)** prints the return on the BIR's own blank with a DRAFT stamp. **Export for eBIRForms** writes the file the offline package loads.
- **Income-tax returns (B44).** The 1701Q and 1702Q take the quarter's sales, cost of sales and itemized deductions from the books, the previous quarters' taxable income and credits from the earlier quarters, and the first three quarters' tax from the filed quarterly returns. The annual 1701, 1701A and 1702-RT take the year's figures the same way; compensation income, the deduction schedules and the NOLCO rows are typed. An individual's Company record needs the individual taxpayer type (single proprietorship or professional) for the 1701 forms.
- **Filing.** Attach the BIR's Tax Return Receipt Confirmation e-mail (the .eml, a PDF print of it, or its text) with Attach BIR confirmation; it is checked against the return (sender, form, TIN, branch, period, received time). Then Submit: the return is filed, read-only, the taxpayer block is kept as it was that day, the clear copy (as filed) is attached, and the figures go to Tax Return Figures for the calendar and the dashboard. A System Manager may file without the e-mail by giving an override reason, which is logged.
- **After filing** nothing changes. A manager opens an amendment (Open amendment, with a reason): a new draft with the same figures, the Amended Return box ticked, linked to the original, which stays filed and readable.

Until a form's engine is built, its return says so under needs and cannot be filed.
