# Runbook: onboarding a client

1. **Site.** `scripts/ops/new_client_site.sh <client>.<domain> <db-root> <admin-pw> <backup-key> <rclone-remote>` on the production bench (Philippine servers, D3). The script sets ph_cas_production, the backup key and remote, ten-year local retention, the encryption key and enables the scheduler.
2. **Company.** Sign in as Administrator, finish the setup wizard with the client's registered name, Philippines, PHP, the fiscal year. Fill the BIR registration section (TIN, 5-digit branch, RDO, VAT status, classification, taxpayer type, top withholding agent flag) and the income-tax options.
3. **Set up Philippine company** (Company form, PH CAS menu): accounts, mapping, the four BIR Series of the head office. Enter the registered range, ACCN and date issued of each series from the Acknowledgment Certificate; add series for other branches.
4. **Masters.** Customers and suppliers with TIN, branch, payee type and name split, flags (government, top withholding agent, RBE, non-resident digital service provider, platform), withholding category for suppliers. Items with VAT class and capital-good flag. Account tax classes for EAR, interest and non-deductible accounts.
5. **Users.** One user per person, roles Accounts User, Accounts Manager, PH Accounting Manager for the signatory; everyone sets up the OTP app at first sign-in. No shared accounts.
6. **Opening balances.** One Journal Entry dated the cut-over day by a PH Accounting Manager (back-dating needs the reason field), checked against the trial balance of the previous system; then create an Accounting Period closing everything before the cut-over.
7. **Settings.** PH CAS Settings: product name (D9), the provider block for the sworn statement, the sign-in threshold. Walk-in customer and collection-receipt switch on the Company.
8. **Registration.** Generate the registration pack (Company form), print to PDF, add the signed sworn statement and file with the RDO. Mark the ACCN and date issued on the Company and on the BIR Series when the AC arrives.
9. **Go-live checks.** Issue one test invoice in a sandbox company, not in the client's company; check the PH Compliance Calendar for the first periods; run `scripts/ops/backup_now.sh` and confirm the upload.
10. **Hand-over.** Give the client the user guide (docs/user-guide.md), the data-processing agreement and the contract (data hand-back, retention, cancellation of registration on termination).
