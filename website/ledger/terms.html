1. The service

MCRC Tax and Accounting Services ("MCRC", "we") provides Sentire Ledger, a hosted computerized accounting system, under one of the plans published on the pricing page, together with the services listed for that plan. The client ("you") is the taxpayer whose books are kept in the system.

2. Term, fees and renewal

Plans are billed annually in advance unless monthly billing is agreed. Fees are VAT exclusive. A plan renews for the same term unless either party gives notice as set out in section 8. Prices are fixed for the paid term; changes apply from the next renewal with 60 days' notice.

3. Your responsibilities

  • The accuracy and completeness of what is entered, and the timely filing and payment of your taxes.
  • Keeping sign-in credentials and two-factor devices safe, and telling us at once about a compromise.
  • Registering the system with your Revenue District Office when the registration pack is ready, and informing the RDO of any later change of system or provider.
  • Using the service within the user, company, branch and volume limits of your plan.

4. Our responsibilities

  • Operating the service with the controls described on this site, including locked books, continuous serials and the audit trail.
  • Daily encrypted backups kept for the retention period, with an off-site copy.
  • Applying changes in tax rules after review by a CPA accredited by the Board of Accountancy, and telling you when a rule you rely on changes.
  • Support within the response times of your plan, during Philippine business hours.

5. Availability

We aim for 99.5 percent monthly availability on all plans, excluding announced maintenance windows and events outside our control. Service credits, where agreed, are set out in the order form.
