Five things to try

Print the sales invoice

Open the posted invoice to Metro Retail Stores and print the PH Sales Invoice: BIR header, serial number, original and duplicate.

Try to delete it

You cannot. Nor can you edit its amounts or back-date a new one. That is the point of a CAS.

Record a collection with withholding

Make a Payment Entry against the invoice; the customer's 2 percent creditable withholding reduces the cash and books the receivable from the BIR.

Run the 2550Q figures

Open PH Tax Return Figures and see the quarter's output and input VAT, item by item of the form.

Look at the audit trail

The BIR Audit Trail report shows every event with its hash chain; the Chain Check proves nothing was altered.
