Privacy notice

Issued under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173) and its implementing rules. Draft for review by counsel. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who we are

MCRC Tax and Accounting Services operates Sentire Ledger and this website. For data in a client's books, the client is the personal information controller and MCRC is the processor (see the data processing terms). For this website, the open demo and our newsletter, MCRC is the controller.

What we collect on this site

  • Demo and pilot requests: name, email, company, mobile number, and the IP address of the request.
  • Newsletter: the email address and name you give us.
  • Server logs needed to run the site, kept 30 days.

We use no advertising trackers.

Why

To send you the demo link and follow up on your request, to send the newsletter you asked for, to protect the service from abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We use infrastructure providers in Singapore and Europe to host the service and send email. They process data on our instructions. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

Demo requests are kept for 24 months, newsletter data until you unsubscribe, and accounting data for as long as the client's agreement and the ten-year retention rules of the BIR require.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to processing, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. Write to mcrc.business.solutions@gmail.com.

Security

Encrypted connections, two-factor sign-in, encrypted backups with keys held separately, access limited to staff who need it, and a tamper-evident audit log of changes in the system.
