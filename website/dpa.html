Data processing terms

Part of the service agreement. Draft for review by counsel. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Roles

The client is the personal information controller for the data in its books (customers, suppliers, employees, TINs, addresses, amounts). MCRC is the personal information processor and acts only on the client's documented instructions, which include the operation of the service as described on the website and in the runbooks.

What MCRC does with the data

  • Stores and processes it to run the client's site, including backups and the nightly restore test on Enterprise standby servers.
  • Accesses it only for support the client asked for, for incident handling, and for the bookkeeping and filing services the client bought.
  • Does not use it for any other purpose, and does not disclose it except to sub-processors below or when the law requires.

Sub-processors

Hosting (Contabo GmbH, Singapore and Germany regions, or another provider named in the order form), email delivery (MCRC's own mail server), off-site backup storage (an object storage bucket named in the order form). Changes are announced 30 days ahead.

Security measures

Encryption in transit and of backups, key escrow shared with the client, two-factor sign-in, role-based access, separate sites per client, daily integrity checks of the audit log, encrypted off-site copies, and the deletion procedure in the offboarding runbook.

Breach notification

MCRC notifies the client without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach, with what is known at the time, so the client can meet its own obligations to the National Privacy Commission and the data subjects.

Assistance

MCRC helps the client answer data subject requests that concern data in the system, and provides the information reasonably needed for the client's privacy impact assessments and registrations.

Return and deletion

At the end of the service MCRC returns the data as a complete export and then deletes it, confirmed by a certificate of deletion, except for records MCRC must keep under its own professional obligations, which are kept only for that purpose and that period.

Audit

The client may ask once a year for the evidence of these measures (backup logs, restore test logs, access lists) and, with reasonable notice, for an audit by an independent party bound by confidentiality.
